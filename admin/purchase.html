    New Purchase Order

    Tips

    Select the worker handling this purchase and the vendor you are buying from. Add all products and quantities you are ordering.

    Receive a Purchase

    Assign each product to a container. Qty will be added to existing inventory.

    Tips

    Select the purchase order that has arrived. Then assign each product to the correct container.

    If the product already exists in the container, the quantity will be added on top of the existing stock.
